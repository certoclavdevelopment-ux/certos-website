In detail

What each engagement actually looks like

Most of our customers implement their product successfully within three months.

Demo

Interested in the Certos offering? Book a free consultation and our team will design a custom demo for your company. The demo is customised to your brand, the equipment you have in production and the features you require. It is an opportunity for your team to ask our technical team anything and get a full view of the Certos value.

Proof of concept

Ready to onboard Certos as your next software partner? A technical proof of concept can be deployed on one or more of your machines. This ensures that, before committing to full-scale development, all your product features and functions can be implemented within Certos.

The proof of concept is also an opportunity to showcase the potential of the new product to your customers, end users and co-decision makers early in the process.

Turnkey solutions

Although Certos covers 90% of what a smart HMI for laboratory equipment needs to do, you still need to customise it to your specific product. Why not let us do it for you? We are very experienced in connecting Certos to the existing microcontroller inside your products.

Certos delivers a full turnkey solution at a fixed price. A simple three-step process gets you to the result:

  • Mock-up — lets you see what your new HMI will look like
  • Proof of concept — a fully functional prototype lets you test our solution with a limited feature set
  • Turnkey solution — we deliver your product innovation
Managed services

Your customers expect a full device management solution with their laboratory equipment, and as a device manufacturer you can provide massive additional value here. Certos has a ready-made solution with CertosCloud: a device management server that runs locally or in the cloud.

We even run it for you, so providing a breakthrough solution to your customers is no hassle. Raw data management, audit trail, predictive maintenance, asset management — it is all there.

Developer support

Certos can be implemented by your in-house team. Or, if we delivered a turnkey solution, you can change and adapt it yourself — for example by coding a custom widget based on your customers’ demands.

We offer full support to your developers to maximise their effectiveness and ensure a smooth implementation process, plus training to get them up to speed on the framework and the latest insights into laboratory equipment IT trends.
